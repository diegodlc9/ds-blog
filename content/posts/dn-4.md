We are excited to announce the new Feedback Inbox feature in our app! This feature allows all users to provide feedback related to the design system, app functionality, or anything else regarding the system. Your feedback is invaluable in helping us continuously improve.

### How to Use the Feedback Inbox

1. **Login to Your Account**: Make sure you are logged into your account.
2. **Locate the Inbox Icon**: Find the inbox icon in the navigation bar.
3. **Try the Beta Version**: Click on the inbox icon to access the beta version of the feedback feature where you can see the feedback messages from users.
4. **Provide Feedback**: In the footer, you will find a button to display the modal where you can submit your feedback.

### What's New?

In addition to the Feedback Inbox feature, we are also redesigning the layout and pages to enhance the overall UI/UX of the app. Stay tuned for more updates!
